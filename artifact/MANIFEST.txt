VeCoDI artifact manifest
=======================

Software
--------
Firmware sources: src/, dummy_partition/, split_inference/, boards/,
CMSIS_NN/, CMakeLists.txt, prj.conf, and tfm_config_overlay.conf.
Host drivers and benchmarks: tools/.

Data and outputs
---------------
The repository includes the test image inputs and representative CSV/JSON
benchmark outputs used by the paper evaluation. New outputs are written to
build/ by the claim runner.

Reproduction entry points
-------------------------
claims/claim1/run.sh reproduces the verified-inference case study on the
supported board. BUILD_FLASH.md documents direct build and flash commands.
