Claim 1: Verified confidential inference on the STM32L552ZE
============================================================

The VeCoDI protocol performs provider attestation, accepts a monotonically
increasing M_update quota, creates an enclave, executes a verified inference,
and destroys the enclave. The claim is reproduced when the run reports:

* EnclaveInfo attestation verified
* M_update accepted
* Enclave created
* Inference result with pred equal to expected
* PoX verified
* Enclave destroyed

This claim covers the case-study workflow and its device-side cycle counters,
not a claim of production provisioning or absolute cycle-count identity.
